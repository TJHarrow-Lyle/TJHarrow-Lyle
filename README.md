# Tyler J. Harrow-Lyle
### Freshwater Ecology | Environmental Modelling | Computational Science

I am a freshwater ecologist working around the intersection of aquatic ecosystem science, environmental modelling, and computational research. My work focuses on understanding the physical, chemical, and biological processes that influence freshwater ecosystems, and developing quantitative approaches to investigate environmental variability, ecosystem dynamics, and ecological responses.

My research integrates field observations, environmental monitoring, statistical modelling, geospatial analysis, process-based approaches, and machine learning to address questions spanning watersheds, tributaries, and lake environments.

## Research Interests

- **Freshwater and Aquatic Ecology:** Ecosystem processes, biological communities, ecological monitoring, and environmental change.
- **Environmental and Physical Modelling:** Hydrodynamics, wave energy, shoreline exposure, and physical drivers of aquatic ecosystems.
- **Watershed and Nutrient Dynamics:** Phosphorus transport, nutrient fate, watershed–lake interactions, and ecological responses.
- **Climate and Environmental Change:** Climate model evaluation, statistical correction, and environmental forcing across the land to lake continuum.
- **Computational Ecology and Machine Learning:** Predictive modelling, ensemble learning, computer vision, deep learning, and automated ecological analysis.
- **Ecological Monitoring and Computer Vision:** Automated image analysis, biological detection, and quantitative assessment of aquatic communities.

## Research and Computational Methods

My work combines ecological theory and environmental observations with computational methods, including:

- Statistical modelling 
- Ensemble machine learning
- Process-based environmental modelling
- Extreme-value analysis and predictive uncertainty
- Geospatial analysis and spatial modelling
- Computer vision and deep learning
- Reproducible analytical workflows in R and Python
